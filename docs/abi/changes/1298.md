#1298: layout_revision 23: rnd::driver::driver_interface, shader_interface, vertex_array_interface and buffer_interface
are pinned. Module code reaches the driver through the inline rnd::render_system::get_driver() and calls its vtable and
the vtables of the shaders, vertex arrays and buffers it creates, so their new constants driver_interface_abi_revision,
shader_interface_abi_revision, vertex_array_interface_abi_revision and buffer_interface_abi_revision (1) join
gs::module_layout_key. No vtable or member changes. swapchain_interface, texture_interface and texture_object_access
stay unpinned: no public engine header hands them to a module. A module built before #1298 is refused until it is
rebuilt.
